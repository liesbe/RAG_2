## Drinking water
Rivermouth tap water is safe to drink unless the town issues a boil-water notice. During a notice, residents should boil water for one full minute before drinking or cooking. The notice status is posted at the town hall and on the official town website.

## Health clinic
The Northbank health clinic is at 42 Cedar Street. It accepts walk-in patients from 8:00 to 11:00 on weekdays. For urgent help after 17:00, residents should call the regional nurse line at 0800 555 014.

## Storm shelter
The designated storm shelter is the lower hall of Rivermouth Town Hall at 3 Harbor Square. It opens when the municipal emergency service issues an evacuation alert. Pets are allowed in carriers, and drinking water is provided.

## Waste collection
Household rubbish is collected on Thursday mornings in the eastern district and Friday mornings in the western district. Bins must be placed at the curb before 7:00. Glass and paper should be taken to the recycling center, not put in household rubbish bins.
