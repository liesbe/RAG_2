## Library hours
Rivermouth Public Library opens Tuesday through Friday from 9:00 to 18:00. On Saturdays it opens from 10:00 to 14:00. It is closed on Sundays and Mondays. The return slot beside the front entrance is available at all hours.

## Recycling center
The Rivermouth recycling center accepts glass, clean paper, and small household batteries. It is open Monday to Friday from 8:30 to 16:30 and Saturday from 9:00 to 12:00. It does not accept paint, medical waste, or large furniture.

## Swimming pool
The Northbank swimming pool is open daily from 6:30 to 20:00. The family swim period is Saturday from 9:00 to 11:00. The pool is closed for maintenance on the first Wednesday of each month.

## Community garden
The community garden is at 18 Willow Lane. Plot holders can collect a key from the library desk on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Watering is allowed before 8:00 or after 19:00 during the summer season.
