## Blue bus
The Blue bus runs between Rivermouth Station and Northbank Hospital every 20 minutes on weekdays. Its first weekday departure from the station is at 6:10. On Sundays, the service runs every hour and starts at 8:00.

## Ferry crossing
The Rivermouth ferry crosses the East Channel in about 12 minutes. It departs every 30 minutes from 7:00 to 19:00 daily. Bicycles travel free, but passengers must buy a ticket before boarding.

## Bicycle repair
The public bicycle repair stand is beside the west entrance of Rivermouth Station. It has a hand pump and basic tools. The stand is available at all times and does not require a booking.

## Winter parking
During snow clearing, overnight parking is prohibited on Market Street from midnight until 6:00. The restriction applies from December 1 through March 15. Vehicles should use the signed car park on Pine Road instead.
